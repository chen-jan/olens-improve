Does AR/FVE selection help on Multihop?

FVE helps a little here: Combine 32 beats 4 random pool bullets by 2.8 points [1.1, 4.8]. Best of 32 is 0.9 points ahead of one random sample, but its interval [−0.1, 2.0] includes zero. And picking the 4 longest bullets, with no AR at all, gets most of the gain (Combine 32 is only 0.8 points ahead [−1.4, 2.9]). Every method also leaves most of the available answers unused: in 31% of cells some sample names every bridge, yet the methods pass only 9.5–13.3% of cells.
