About this data

What a case is

Qwen3.6-27B answered 149 real chat prompts. Along each reply we picked read sites (ends of clauses, sentences, markup, line breaks, quotes), 1,123 in all, and took the model's activation there at five layers: 20, 36, 44, 52 and 60. That gives 5,615 cases. For each case the Oracle Lens (O-lens) wrote readouts of that activation as short bullets, and three ways of choosing the bullets were judged.

The three settings

  • Original, capped to four bullets (Original): the lens's first sample, cut to its first four bullets (and to 256 tokens).
  • Choose the best complete readout (32 samples) (Best of 32): of 32 complete samples, the one whose bullets together rebuild the activation best (highest joint FVE).
  • Combine original bullets (32 samples) (Combine 32): bullets pooled from all 32 samples; up to four are picked greedily by FVE gain, then one swap pass. Its bullets can come from different samples.
  • Released lens, raw output (reference column): the released lens output before the four-bullet cap, judged the same way. It has no FVE numbers.

What FVE is

The activation reconstructor (AR) turns each bullet into a vector. The target activation and the bullet vectors are whitened with one whitener per layer (pooled, ridge 0.1), the bullet vectors are scaled to unit length, and non-negative least squares (NNLS) finds the mix of bullets closest to the target. Joint FVE = 1 − |residual|² / |target|². 1 means the bullets rebuild the activation exactly; 0 means they explain none of it.

Per-bullet scores

  • Shapley share: this bullet's fair share of the readout's FVE. The shares of one readout add up to 100%.
  • Shapley: the same in FVE units, averaged over every order of adding bullets (the empty set scores 0). The values add up to the joint FVE.
  • Solo: FVE of this bullet alone.
  • Drop: how much the joint FVE falls when this bullet is removed and the others are refitted.
  • NNLS coefficient: the bullet's weight in the joint fit. Repeated bullets share one vector, so their weights are not unique and their Shapley credit is split equally.

How the judge labels readouts

The project judge reads the readout against the model's own reply, before and after the read point. That reply is the ground truth.

  • Hallucinated: at least one verified wrong span.
  • Off-topic: a verified off-topic span and no wrong span.
  • Generic: the judge found nothing specific to check (or the readout was empty).
  • Consistent: everything else.
  • A span counts as verified only if its text (ignoring whitespace, quote style and case) occurs in the readout and is at least 3 characters long. Unverified spans are shown with a dotted underline and do not count.
  • A second stage sorts the other claims into true, unverifiable, disputed and off-topic.
  • Bullet labels come from where the spans land: wrong if a verified wrong span overlaps the bullet, off-topic if only an off-topic span does, clean otherwise. Bullets in generic or empty readouts get no label.

Limits to keep in mind

  • The AR reads only the first 32 tokens of each bullet. Greyed text after that point is shown but does not change any number.
  • A low score is not a truth label. A correct bullet can explain little of the activation and a wrong one can explain a lot.
  • The judge reused verdicts for identical texts across settings, so labels for shared bullets are not independent.
  • Everything here is exploratory: all 149 chats were looked at before, including the test split.
  • � (the Unicode replacement character) is part of the lens output itself: it marks bytes the lens wrote that are not valid text. It is shown as written, so highlights still line up.

Where the numbers come from

Readouts, joint FVE and the 32 Best-of-32 candidate scores come from the saved selections of the expanded O-lens improvement run. Per-bullet scores were recomputed from the saved AR vectors with the same whitening and solver, and the recomputed joint FVE matches the saved one. Judge labels, spans and claims are the saved project-judge results for each setting. The overview statistics come from the summary file (a bootstrap over chats) when it loads; otherwise, and when the overview is limited to the filtered cases, they are computed in your browser from the case index.

    Reading study and errors

    The Reading study section summarises a hand reading of 288 bullets drawn in 7 strata (by judge label and Shapley share). The Errors section adds 240 judge-verified wrong bullets drawn at random and read by hand, the recurrence of every verified wrong span across the cell’s 32 lens samples, and 72 development cells in which all 32 samples were judged. Inside a case, the reader notes, the “How avoidable is this cell?” panel and the recurrence lines under each wrong span come from these analyses. Readers are not ground truth, and the recurrence counts are word matches, not meaning matches.
