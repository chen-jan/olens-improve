# Delivery checklist

Checkpoint: 2026-10-10, after complete validation and the final six-arm TEST freeze. This is an evidence and handoff checklist, not authority to launch jobs or open another cohort. [STATUS.md](STATUS.md) and immutable run records govern current execution. No TEST quality results were read to prepare this file.

**Complete** means the stated measurement or audit finished, not that the method works. **Incomplete** means an intended measurement remains unfinished. **Audit failed** means a concrete contract failed; it does not make an unmeasured quality result negative. **Not run** means a proposed variant was not measured. Engineering checks, diagnostic scores and benchmark quality are separate evidence.

## Combinations and scientific controls

Measured pilot combinations include D→pruning, Emean→pruning, edited output→pruning, exact repeat support plus D16, pooled specificity selection, pooled specificity→pruning, and an Emean nonpositive-objective abstention control. Their parents, objective/component-off controls and applicable matched-count random deletions are preserved in [011](experiments/011-cached-combinations.md) and [020](experiments/020-pooled-specificity.md). This was a bounded adaptive search, not an exhaustive cross-product.

The twelve-arm validation comparison is complete. The final menu is exactly raw N1, capped N1, A16, A32, random N32 and Emean32. A16/Emean32 are experimental operating points; A32 provides the specificity-off and sample-budget comparison. No combined method was selected. [032](experiments/032-test-final-method-freeze.md) fixes the choice, exact-source contracts and final endpoints before TEST quality access. Later DEV results cannot change that menu.
