## Procedure and bounds

1. Parse the original candidates using the existing four-bullet/256-Qwen-token policy. Parser-uncertain candidates remain excluded, exactly as in the prior B pilot. A none-effort Standard Luna helper proposes zero, one or two **self-contained, contiguous, verbatim** spans per source bullet. At most 32 bullets enter one extraction chunk, with 6,000 maximum completion tokens. The entire eligible N16 pool therefore needs at most two extraction calls. A whole-bullet fallback is allowed only if self-contained; fragments are rejected rather than rewritten.
2. The caller finds each exact quote's unique occurrence in its original bullet and computes bullet-relative and full-readout offsets. Altered quotes, ambiguous repeats, empty quotes and excess proposals are rejected; no text is silently repaired. An exact substring can still drop a governing condition, negation, modality, time restriction, quoted/reporting scope or entity binding.
3. A **separate medium-effort Standard Luna check** sees the full parent bullet and the exact proposed span. Source entailment, self-containedness and qualification preservation must all be explicitly true before that span receives any vote. False/null/missing checks exclude the span and remain visible in the artifact. Verification chunks contain at most 16 spans, with 6,000 completion tokens including reasoning: at most eight chunks per cell. These labels establish candidate-text support only, not factual correctness or activation content.
4. Exact whitespace-normalized span groups use deterministic first-source verbatim representatives. A bounded lexical shortlist proposes at most 40 nonexact cross-sample group pairs per cell; the medium matcher confirms them in chunks of at most 20, again with 6,000 completion tokens. Lexical overlap retrieves pairs, never decides semantics; translations and distant paraphrases can be missed. Bidirectional equivalence and all-member compatibility are required for clustering. One-way entailment never merges groups, and unchecked/uncertain pairs remain separate. There are at most two confirmation chunks.
5. Rank exact or semantic groups by the number of **distinct source samples**, with at most one vote per sample even if it supplies duplicate/overlapping spans. A vote originates from a separately accepted full-source→span relation; span equivalence connects these supported propositions. Use only soft ranking (no minimum count) and support fraction `.125` (two of 16 samples). Retain at most four unchanged representative spans and 256 Qwen tokens. No AR score is claimed for these new spans.

There are at most twelve logical helper calls per cell, one logical attempt per call, four shared request slots and two queued cells. Provider retries, uncertain charges and cache-write costs remain subject to the central ledger and must be counted; twelve is not a guarantee about physical billed attempts. None/medium helper roles and prompts are frozen; the sole benchmark judge remains Luna with effort none. A separate context-aware reader is not part of this pilot.

## Controls and attribution

Export ten conditions on exactly the same completed cohort: unchanged A16; unchanged whole-bullet B soft and `.125` compatibility-off/veto-off controls; extraction-only A16; exact span consensus soft and `.125`; semantic span consensus soft and `.125`; and a seeded random-span control matching the count of each semantic output. Extraction-only A16 retains the parent sample's source-bullet/span order and drops only complete trailing spans to meet the shared cap. Random controls use the same **all-N16 accepted-span pool**, exact deduplication and caps; unsuccessful count matching is explicitly flagged rather than described as matched. Token lengths remain reported because equal count is not equal length.

Extraction-only isolates changes to A16's granularity, while random all-pool controls help distinguish expanded selection opportunities from support ranking. Neither is a complete causal decomposition: qualification filtering and granularity jointly alter availability. Whole-bullet controls preserve the strict original definition. This pilot does not infer that a supported or repeated claim is true, nor that omitting a claim means the activation lacks it.
