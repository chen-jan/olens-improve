## Measured comparison and interpretation

The target activation matters for these recovery instruments: capped N1 recovered the required multi-hop bridge in **29/341 cells**, versus **0/341** with matched donor text; chain's top-1 intermediate was **26/330 versus 10/330**; complete role binding was **151/3,420 versus 0/3,420**. Any-cell item recovery was respectively **11/31 versus 0/31**, **15/30 versus 8/30**, and **23/30 versus 0/30**. These are different aggregations: more layers/sites offer more chances for an item hit. They do not verify extra facts or prove causal faithfulness.

The hallucination control shows why wrong-rate reduction alone is insufficient. Target N1 had **543 wrong, 148 off-topic, 446 consistent and 8 generic** outputs. Donors had **25 wrong, 956 off-topic, 11 consistent and 153 generic**. The headline wrong/(wrong+off-topic+consistent) falls from **47.76% to 2.52%**, while off-topic output rises from **12.93% to 83.49%** and consistent output falls from **38.95% to 0.96%**. Secondary wrong/(wrong+consistent) worsens from **54.90% to 69.44%**. The donor has avoided the target content; this is not improved truthful recovery. Assertion shares, off-topic counts and consistent coverage must accompany any main error decrease.

Paired uncertainty resamples whole target related groups, 2,000 draws with seed 20261010. For cell recovery, donor-minus-target deltas are −.0850 for multi-hop, interval [−.1705,−.0219]; −.0485 for chain, interval [−.1240,−.0057]; and −.0442 for role, interval [−.0614,−.0281]. Chain has only seven independent groups. These intervals condition on the fixed donor assignment: reuse of 3,362 donor cells couples some targets and is not fully resampled by target-group bootstrap. They should not be read as universal donor-control power guarantees.
