## Completed pool-versus-ranking diagnostic

For the same 189 development cells, candidate availability exceeds selected recovery: multi-hop has an instrument-passing candidate in **14/24 cells**, but A32 selects one in **7/24**; chain has **9/21 available versus 3/21 selected**; role binding has **20/72 versus 9/72**. Exact uniform random expectation, retaining original sample multiplicity, is 6.09375, 0.6875 and 8.65625 passing cells respectively. Many cells also have no passing candidate: 10 multi-hop, 12 chain and 52 role. These are separate generation and selection failures. Availability is a noisy, judge-backed optimistic diagnostic; it never enters inference selection.

For hallucination, 54/72 cells contain a consistent-asserting candidate. A32 selects 24 consistent, 39 wrong, 8 off-topic and 1 generic output. Its wrong/asserting rate 39/71 is close to the random ratio of expected counts 40.0625/71.0625. The paired difference is −.0145, target-group bootstrap interval [−.0910,.0653]. Cached duplicate verdicts retain sample multiplicity without becoming independent judge repeats.

Within-cell pairwise FVE discrimination is weak overall: pooled good-versus-bad rank rates are .541 multi-hop, .502 chain, .538 role and .535 hallucination, with ties counted as half. For hallucination this compares wrong versus other asserting classes, including off-topic, rather than claiming all nonwrong outputs are useful. The consistent-versus-wrong pair rate is .533. Equal-cell means are .496, .487, .518 and .541; the difference reflects unequal numbers of eligible good/bad pairs. All related-group intervals for excess over .5 include zero. This is uncertainty, not a significance stop rule. The chain sample has only two independent groups; all-good/all-bad/undefined cells remain explicitly separate.

A numerical benefit can coexist with weak aggregate ranking: chain A32's 3 selected hits exceed the .6875 random expectation, despite overall pairwise discrimination near chance. Conversely, an available hit does not certify a good full readout. At role cell `oa-father-daughter-worry-ba L44 p135`, sample 0 passes daughter→father worry but invents several names and illness details; higher-FVE sample 22 fails the binding. The actual scene concerns Peter/Nora. At multi-hop `mhh-wohler-berzelius L60 p38`, highest-FVE sample 8 (.6564) misses Wöhler, while sample 31 (.5207) names Friedrich Wöhler amid other claims. These are recovery instruments, not precision for every emitted fact. The numerical and hallucination traces in the artifact retain complete texts and labels for inspection.
