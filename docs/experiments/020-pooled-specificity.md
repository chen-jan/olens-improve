## Inspected evidence and alternative explanations

Examples were selected deterministically by cell ID for wins/regressions, plus seeded unchanged examples; the artifact preserves full baseline/new texts, judge evidence and source scores. Two role instrument wins at `oa-comedian-heckler-humiliate-ab L60 p178` and `...-ba L60 p160` follow deletion of one bullet. In the first, the judge switches from “action unspecified” to inferring humiliation from the same retained “scathing, witty comeback”; in the second, it changes agent extraction after deletion. These suggest that removing distracting alternatives may help extraction, but surviving invented names and details are not validated. This mechanism is a hypothesis.

Two MT regressions are concrete deletions of required bridge strings: `mhh-1967-norwegian-classes-objects-codesigner L60 p28` loses the “Simula 67” bullet, and `mhh-mesopotamian-wildman-cedar L60 p46` loses the “Enkidu” bullet. Conversely, the pooled gain at `mhh-simula-norsk-regnesentral L60 p33` says “Simula Research Laboratory”; the frozen required-bridge alias is bare “Simula”. That string match alone does not establish recovery of the language rather than a laboratory name. At `chain-w2-d2p-0108 L44 p63`, 15 remains in the pruned output but extracted values reorder from [15,3,17] to [3,15,17], losing the top-value instrument pass. This is extraction sensitivity rather than disappearance of the correct number.

Hallucination examples also limit causal interpretation. At `chat-lmsys-0026 L20 p471`, pruning removes one bullet but retains two formerly wrong spans, including “mandatory RNAV routes”; the verdict changes wrong→consistent with zero true and five unverifiable claims. At `chat-dailydialog-0027 L20 p123`, pooling retains the formerly flagged “draft a packing list” span while changing wrong→consistent. Preserve the official scores, but these do not demonstrate removal of the alleged contradiction. A seeded unchanged `chat-lmsys-0005 L44 p291` example is consistent under both methods despite zero true claims and four/five unverifiable claims. Low contradiction and recovered useful information must remain separate.

Decision: retain this combination as a measured pilot, not a promoted broad method. The simpler E1→pruning arm already scheduled for the full development grid has better aggregate pilot coverage; pooling+specificity does not justify another full-grid arm yet. A one-family gain can still motivate follow-up when its mechanism, cost and uncertainty make that worthwhile. Negative and judge-sensitive findings remain useful rather than proving the method cannot work.
