## What was measured

The final comparison covers twelve fixed methods on all four selected families: 268 held-out items and 10,714 activation sites. All 469 items and 19,435 sites are also reported as a separate descriptive union of development, validation and test. Development includes all six nested sampling budgets from 1 to 32. The wider WorkspaceBench suite remains a separate follow-up.

No clear improvement across all four tasks was established. Best-of-16 recovers three more hidden-name questions than the original lens, with broad uncertainty. Comparing other activations adds one question over best-of-32. Pruning lowers judged errors but also loses information; matched random deletion often performs similarly or better. Required names, numbers or role answers can occur inside an otherwise incorrect explanation. Official verdicts remain unchanged after example audits.

The seven approach categories have different levels of evidence. Consensus and editing have bounded judged development pilots; alternate readers have diagnostic evidence and unresolved prerequisites. They were not all promoted to the final twelve-method comparison. [The report](REPORT.md) and [checklist](DELIVERY_CHECKLIST.md) distinguish completed tests, failed implementation contracts and optional unrun variants.
