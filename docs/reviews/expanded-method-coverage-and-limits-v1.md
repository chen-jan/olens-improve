## Reconstruction and interpretation limits

The AR sees only the **first 32 cleaned tokens per bullet**. The full emitted string and scored prefix have separate provenance. Fresh encoding of an edit does not supply evidence for its unseen suffix. Different full strings can share an effective AR input; equal cache keys establish computational reuse, not semantic equivalence.

The source-bound all-candidate diagnostic `diagnostics/all-candidate-scores-v1/quality-analysis-v2.json` found 1,418/6,048 eligible candidates with a truncated scoring atom, but **no complete-readout effective-key multiset collisions with different full texts** in that sample. Thus the visibility limitation is real; that diagnostic did not demonstrate a full-readout collision or establish that truncation caused the ranking errors. [Experiment 012](../experiments/012-all-candidate-diagnostic.md) keeps availability versus ranking separate.

Fixed B128 is the chosen later implementation, not established as equivalent to B32. `diagnostics/ar-batch-expanded-v1/{detailed-comparison.json,selector-sensitivity.json}` retains failed provisional equivalence guards: maximum FVE drift .003524, D32 text changes in 2/12 cells and mean-specificity change in 1/12. Tiny score improvements and .001 pruning boundaries therefore deserve explicit numerical-sensitivity limits. No production vectors were replaced by that audit. Reconstruction fit is not factual truth; recovered names, numbers or role answers can coexist with wrong surrounding prose. Helpers and judges sharing Luna are not independent semantic evidence.
