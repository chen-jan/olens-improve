**Consensus settings differ materially.** Compatibility at support zero retains 16 hidden-name and 60 role questions (ordinary), versus 14 and 59 (medium), while the repeated-support .125 setting retains 5 and 21 (ordinary), versus 5 and 6 (medium). Unfiltered support-zero semantic variants retain 33–34 hidden-name and 78 role questions. Their quality remains uncertain against the exact support-zero reference. The compatibility/support filters trade retained information for lower contradiction rates; the .125 results must not represent every semantic variant.

**Likelihood is a separate experiment.** True-minus-two-control / own / control N8 select 34/36/42 hidden-name questions, 48/53/56 arithmetic questions, and 82/77/79 role questions, respectively. Their transcript contradiction rates are 47.763/46.225/47.063%. Every recorded capped-N1 joint quality interval crosses zero. Their reconstructed fit stays close to capped N1; likelihood selection does not directly maximize that fit. There is no recorded pairwise own-vs-control-vs-difference quality interval, so these marginal differences do not demonstrate activation specificity.

**Incremental editing, not inherited fit.** R2-versus-R1 paired fit gains are +0.005/+0.002/+0.035/+0.001 pp for transcripts/hidden names/arithmetic/roles. All three recovery counts remain the same across R1, R2 and pooling. Arithmetic R2 versus pooling has 163 improved, zero worsened and 1,157 neutral sites at the approved 1e-5 tolerance; raw means and intervals are retained. Two duplicate recorded pooling-parent entries with different provenance kinds are not two independent gains.

**Pruning controls.** A32 .005 pruning versus matched-count random deletion changes transcript contradiction rate +2.052 pp (pointwise interval positive; joint interval crosses zero), hidden names −3 questions, arithmetic −11 questions, and roles −3 questions. Edited .005 pruning versus its matched deletion has joint intervals crossing zero in all four tasks. Matched deletion controls bullet count, not exact token length. The clear transcript improvement against an unpruned parent is not evidence that the reconstruction-guided choice beats arbitrary shortening.

**Reviewed prose explains limits, not effect sizes.** The final agent_3 review binds 28 full-context units, 61 unique text/official-evidence comparisons and 115 reference-specific cases. Edited arithmetic and Adwa anchors can be byte-identical to pooling; observed wrong arithmetic or optional answer names then cannot be attributed to the editor. Semantic role filtering can retain a correct reversed-action clause while adding unsupported coercion/food details. Transcript class flips can retain the flagged DETR variant or rest on unsupported creator/home assumptions. Preserve the official endpoint; identify these as local content limits or judge disagreements, not score corrections. A whole-question pass may survive only at other grid sites, so selected anchor text cannot explain every item-level change.

**Delivery limits.** No global winner follows from these tasks. No pooled average, independent reader, newly held-out claim, or mechanism validation is established. Raw N1 has no comparable saved reconstruction fit; missing/invalid atom fits remain unavailable.
