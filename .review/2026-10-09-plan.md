## Findings and adopted resolutions

### 1. An inconclusive pilot could cause premature rejection

**Original location:** Execution stages and decisions, lines 89–94.

The plan allowed expansion when uncertainty warranted it, but also instructed stopping for “weak gains within uncertainty.” These conflict. The proposed 20-item validation splits, including only ten independent role pairs, cannot reliably exclude small worthwhile effects.

**Adopted resolution:** Pilots establish instrument validity, cost and plausible effect size. Lack of statistical significance alone is not a stopping rule. Freeze practical effect tolerances and uncertainty rules before validation; stop for concrete failures, excessive cost or a defined futility condition. Disclose uncertainty rather than interpreting an underpowered null as evidence of no benefit.

### 2. A universal layer instruction contradicted family-specific grids

**Original location:** Objective and frozen contract, line 21; evaluation design, line 55.

The instruction to use layers 20–60 in steps of four conflicted with the selected families' actual grids. Extra layers would change both any-cell success opportunities and costs.

**Adopted resolution:** Explicitly freeze hallucination at `20,36,44,52,60`, role-bound at `20,28,36,44,52,60`, and multihop MT/chain at `20–60 step 4`. Chain retains its default final-token site. Preset development subsets must be identical across compared arms and clearly distinguished from the full evaluation.

### 3. Unsupported content exceeded what the headline measures

**Original location:** Evaluation design, lines 48 and 59.

The plan described “unsupported” content without explicitly requiring both hallucination judging stages. Stage 1 detects transcript contradictions and intentionally permits compatible unmentioned content; it cannot identify every false or activation-unsupported claim.

**Adopted resolution:** Name the primary metric transcript contradiction and require stage 2 for main/final hallucination comparisons. Report headline `W/(W+O+C)`, assertion share `(W+O+C)/all readouts`, and secondary `W/(W+C)`, where W/O/C are wrong/off-topic/consistent readouts. Report stage-2 coverage and true/false/unverifiable tallies. Preserve the distinction between established wrong spans and independently segmented atomic claims, and between unverifiability and falsehood.

### 4. The output cap remained optional

**Original location:** Evaluation design, line 59; baselines, line 63.

“Where possible” budget matching and a conditional four-bullet cap could credit additional claims or verbosity as better selection.

**Adopted resolution:** Every proposed final output has at most four bullets. Preserve the untouched released N=1 baseline and add a deterministic same-cap N=1 reference. Freeze cap/token/claim handling after the length pilot and before method comparisons. Use matched-length/deletion controls where relevant and report residual length differences.

### 5. Conditional pilots weakened the promise to examine all seven methods

**Original location:** Methods, line 67; execution stages, lines 90–91.

The plan promised a bounded pilot for every top-level method but could skip D when prior methods did not reveal complementarity. It also proposed F/G only on diagnosed failures, which cannot estimate general benefit.

**Adopted resolution:** Give D a small representative development pilot. Separate targeted failure-case diagnostics for F/G from a shared random development comparison. Document concrete unavailable prerequisites separately from negative findings. Use inexpensive N=2 and N=4 prefixes of existing pools. Expand promising methods without requiring every variant to receive a full run.

## 2026-10-09 addendum: quality-first scaling and execution target

**Review status:** Requirements addressed in the completed incremental plan revision; final recheck recorded below. This addendum preserves the original findings and their historical resolution above.

The user prioritizes maximum quality, accepts a small recovery loss subject to judgment, and prefers broadly useful methods. There is no N=8 cap: sampling may expand from 32 to 64/128 when measured benefit and time justify it. The work should examine combinations with component ablations, communicate key facts to newcomers, target a 20-hour execution window, and adapt judging speed and diagnostic phases to observed bottlenecks.

### Incremental risks and required resolutions

1. **An apparent frontier can be selection noise.** Repeatedly choosing N, thresholds and combinations from noisy results inflates apparent gains. Freeze escalation and operating-point rules on development before test; report paired uncertainty and the range actually measured. A flat or increasing curve across a few N values does not establish a scaling law. Repeated test inspection remains prohibited.

2. **A cross-family plot is not a within-item tradeoff.** Hallucination errors and recovery in separate benchmark families come from different cohorts. Label them explicitly, give family-level denominators and uncertainty, and avoid suggesting one removed claim caused one lost recovery. A claim of broad usefulness needs evidence across families; a localized gain must remain labeled as such.

3. **A combination may win through extra resources.** Compare each component alone and the combination with the same candidate pool and final output budget. Also report equal-compute comparisons where feasible. Distinguish better selection from gains caused by additional sampling, editing or scoring.

4. **API dollars, GPU-hours and elapsed latency constrain different things.** Report all three separately, with phase forecasts against the 20-hour target. Record the measured bottleneck and before/after throughput and correctness checks for optimizations. Faster judging can release time for diagnostics; it does not establish that model generation or reconstruction will finish on time.

5. **The time target cannot turn unrun methods into negative results.** Continue representative coverage of all seven runnable approaches where feasible, but classify anything omitted because the execution window ended as incomplete/time-limited. A missing technical dependency is a prerequisite block; a method that was never run has no empirical failure result. Reforecast after the baseline throughput measurement and preserve the exact unfinished work in the handoff.

6. **The report must explain the practical finding first.** Lead prose and dashboards with what improved versus the released lens, measured effect and uncertainty, breadth of evaluation, elapsed time/spend, current status, and the main limitation. Define terms in plain language; distinguish observations, hypotheses and planned work. A dashboard does not substitute for raw evidence and reproducible manifests.

The experiment template now captures these points. No inference, paid judging, GPU work or new external review was performed for this addendum.

**Judging and timing clarification:** The 20-hour target begins at actual execution, not documentation. Use Flex by default with bounded concurrency, exact-input caching, measured latency/throughput, backoff and outstanding-cost reservations. If judging blocks the critical path, a logged, bounded Standard-tier fallback must preserve the Luna model, effort and prompts and stay within the authorized budget. This is not a judge substitution or a speed guarantee. Timeouts do not establish zero charges; retain uncertain reservations until reconciled.

## 2026-10-09 decision addendum: initial sample cap and helper policy

This later decision supersedes the earlier high-N expansion and general Flex-default notes.
